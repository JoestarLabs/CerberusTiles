<p><i>Cerberus Tiles</i> adds essential Quick Settings tiles and app shortcuts to Google Pixel devices, providing fast access to core settings that aren't readily available by default. Ideal for use with back tap gestures.</p><p><br><b>Features:</b></p><ul><li><b>Adaptive Brightness:</b> In-app toggle, app shortcut, and Quick Settings tile for quick display brightness automation</li><li><b>Brightness Presets:</b> Instant adjustment between preset brightness levels (0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%)</li><li><b>Vibration & Ringer Mode:</b> Switch between Normal, Vibrate, and Silent ringer modes with DND protection</li><li><b>Limit Charge to 80% (Charging Optimization):</b> In-app toggle to limit charging to 80% or toggle optimization modes to prolong battery lifespan (requires one-time ADB permission)</li><li><b>Network Speed Limit:</b> Quick Settings tile and in-app controls to rate-limit download bandwidth (requires one-time ADB permission)</li><li><b>Smart Pixels:</b> Hardware-accelerated true-black OLED pixel mesh to reduce battery consumption, with adjustable intensity (10% to 90%) and automatic pause during casting or screen recording (Android 15+)</li><li><b>Overlay Dialog:</b> Convenient app shortcut to access essential controls over any application</li><li><b>Component Customization:</b> Tailor your experience by choosing which controls and tiles appear in the app</li></ul><p><br><b>Permissions & Privacy:</b></p><p>Cerberus Tiles operates with <b>zero internet access</b>, <b>zero tracking</b>, and <b>zero analytics</b>. All permissions are strictly optional and requested only when enabling corresponding features.</p><p>The app fills the gap where Google Pixel phones lack quick access to core system settings, offering a lightweight, power-user solution.</p>
